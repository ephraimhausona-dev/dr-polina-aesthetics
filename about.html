Our Story

Care that starts with understanding your skin

Dr. Polina Aesthetics was created to take the guesswork out of skincare. Too many people try product after product without ever understanding what their skin actually needs — we start every relationship with a proper consultation instead.

Whether you're dealing with acne, rosacea, premature ageing, sun damage or pigmentation, your plan is built around your skin type — dry, normal, oily or sensitive — and adjusted as your skin changes.

What Guides Us

Our approach to care

Three principles shape every consultation and treatment plan at Dr. Polina Aesthetics.

Personalised, always

No one-size-fits-all routines — every plan is built around your specific skin type and goals.

Medically guided

Treatments are grounded in clinical knowledge, not trends — safe, considered, and explained clearly.

A calm, welcoming space

Skincare can feel overwhelming — our clinic is designed to make it feel simple and judgement-free.
