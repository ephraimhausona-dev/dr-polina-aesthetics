Dr. Polina Aesthetics is a medical aesthetic center that tailors skin, hair and body treatments to the individual needs of each client.

See treatmentsVisit a clinic

Two clinics,
in Windhoek
and Oshikango.

We started the practice to create skincare and wellness awareness.

All treatments are tailored to address skin concerns depending on the skin type, whether dry, normal, oily or sensitive, and on concerns such as acne, rosacea, premature ageing, sun damage and pigmentation.

We also address the extraction of congested pores, blackheads, whiteheads and ingrown hairs, and the exfoliation of dead skin cells.
