# Dr. Polina Aesthetics, second build (from scratch)

Self-authored under explicit creative delegation. The client rejected build one as
"still too basic, too close to her old one" and asked for a new site from scratch:
same theme (black, light grey, a touch of light pink), unique, 3D, slideshows,
mouse interaction, "absolutely 10/10". Content and imagery stay sourced from
drpolinaaesthetics.com only.

## The eight topics
1. Vibe: fashion magazine cover meets clinic glass. Vogue type, serum, light through liquid.
   References: a Bodoni masthead, a drop of serum under a macro lens, a frosted clinic door.
2. Journey: meet her through the drop, read what the practice is, turn the treatments
   in your hand, see the facial balancing result through a lens, visit the clinics,
   pick products, hear a client, write your own consultation request.
3. Energy: loud open (3D), quiet read, playful ring, intense lens, calm clinics,
   tactile shop, warm review, decisive close.
4. Feeling curve below. Peak: the hero drop.
5. Signature: a liquid serum drop in real 3D that you can touch. It dents under the
   cursor, ripples on click, and refracts her portrait; scrolling flies the camera
   through floating clinic photographs until you see her through the drop.
6. Range: editorial luxury, not premium minimal. High contrast serif, big italics.
7. Distinct scenes, not one world. Only the hero is a 3D space.
8. Assets: her site's photography only (portrait cutout, two studio portraits,
   clinic photos, treatment, community, before and after, product shots).

## Feeling curve
| Act | Feeling | Cause |
|---|---|---|
| Hero | wonder | a glass drop that reacts to touch, her face bending through it |
| Manifesto | calm attention | one sentence filling in word by word |
| Ring | play | a 3D carousel of concerns you can spin |
| Facial balancing | curiosity, then proof | a lens that shows the after wherever you look |
| Clinics | trust | real rooms flipping in 3D strips, addresses, hours |
| Shop | want | products that turn under the mouse |
| Words | warmth | a client's words lit by your cursor |
| Consultation | agency | your choices written onto an appointment card |
| Footer | signature | her name reacting to the cursor |

Peak: "you can poke the drop and it bends her face." Lives in the hero, the largest span (260vh).
Tell someone: "It's the site where you touch a drop of serum and it ripples."

## Grammar and fingerprint
Grammar: object led editorial (a single 3D object opens, then magazine spreads, each
with its own interaction). Nav: centred masthead wordmark, menu left, bag and book
right, header ink follows section theme. Hero: WebGL transmission object over a
photographic plane with floating photo planes. Close: appointment card builder.
Differs from build one (filmic consultation, margin rail, CSS layered cutout, balance
line, appointment card stage) on grammar, nav, hero device, sequence, signature.
