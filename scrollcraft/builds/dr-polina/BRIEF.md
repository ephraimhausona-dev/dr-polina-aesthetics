# Dr. Polina Aesthetics: brief

**Self-authored under explicit creative delegation.** The owner relayed the
client's verdict and gave direction; everything not covered below is an authored
decision, labelled as such.

## What the client said (relayed, verbatim)

> She doesnt like the website. She said it is too basic and looks like a generic
> template.

## Direction given (verbatim)

> make it look like a 10000 dollar website with slideshows and animations. the
> pink is too dominating. the theme is black, light grey with a touch of light
> pink. do not make the site look generic and AI designed. also add a shop for
> people to buy products and at checkout they are redirected to whatsapp for
> purchasing with the product, price and everything already included to make it
> seemless for the client. also do not use any - in the site ... do not
> hilucinate. everything should be sourced from her original site

## The eight topics

1. **Vibe** (authored): fashion editorial clinic. Black and stone grey, one
   blush line. References: a fashion magazine cover where the model stands in
   front of the masthead; a clinic appointment card; gallery wall labels.
2. **Journey** (authored, built from her own site's order: welcome, about,
   services, shop, Instagram, reviews, visit us): portrait, your skin, what we
   treat, the clinics, facial balancing proof, shop, kind words and community,
   visit and book.
3. **Energy**: calm open, rising through the concerns, quiet in the clinic
   slideshow, loudest at facial balancing, warm and low at the close.
4. **Feeling**: see the curve below. The one moment: the line sweeping across
   the face.
5. **The one thing**: a single blush line that you drag across a real before
   and after, ticking the treatments as it passes.
6. **Range**: premium minimal pushed toward editorial fashion (oversized type,
   hard ground cuts), not spa pastel.
7. **One world or scenes**: distinct scenes, hard cuts between black and grey.
8. **Assets**: only what exists on drpolinaaesthetics.com. Two studio portraits,
   two clinic portraits, Instagram feed images, blog before and after images,
   product photos. No generated imagery. The first studio portrait was cut out
   locally (flood fill on the even backdrop) for the layered hero.

## Content rules

* Every claim, name, price, address and policy comes from her live site.
* No hyphen, en dash or em dash in visible copy.
* No invented numbers. No counters.
* Products show the stock status her shop shows.

## Feeling curve (home)

1. Presence: she stands in front of her own name, the name recedes as you move.
2. Recognition: your skin type named plainly on a grey page.
3. Tension: the concerns arrive one at a time while the frame holds.
4. Calm: the clinic itself, photographs dissolving slowly into each other.
5. Awe (peak): the blush line crosses the profile and the change is visible.
6. Appetite: the products slide past sideways.
7. Warmth: a client's words and the clinic's community work.
8. Resolve: an appointment card. Two addresses, the hours, one way to book.

## Peak

"There's this line you drag across the face and it shows you the before and
after, and ticks off each treatment." Lives in act 5, largest span on the page.

## Tell someone

It's the site where you pull a line across a real face and watch the treatment
happen.

## Authored silence

None. The clinic slideshow is calm, not empty: it always shows a photograph.
