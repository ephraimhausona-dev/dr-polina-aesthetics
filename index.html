New 2026 signature service

Facial
balancing.

“In modern aesthetic medicine, the goal is no longer to change faces, it is to restore balance, structure, and harmony.”
  1. BotoxRelaxes the muscle activity behind fine lines.
  2. Dermal fillersRestores lost volume, such as the cheeks and mid face.
  3. Thread liftingFor loss of lift and definition.
  4. Chin enhancementImproves chin projection and lower face balance.
  5. Smile linesNasolabial fold correction.
  6. Under eye correctionHollows treated with fillers or skin rejuvenating treatments.

All facial balancing treatments are fully customised. Procedures and products are tailored after a detailed medical facial assessment. Not every client requires every treatment.
