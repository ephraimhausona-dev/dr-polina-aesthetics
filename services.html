From the journal

Why is having a prescribing practitioner essential?

Filler, especially in lip enhancements, should be placed by someone trained to prevent and manage complications.

  • Overfilled or misplaced filler. When too much is used or it is injected at an incorrect depth, causing an unnatural appearance.
  • Infections or reactions. Rare, but poor sterility can lead to infection at the injection site.
  • Accidental vascular injection. Very rare, yet a medical emergency if not spotted and dealt with correctly. Practitioners should have specific training in using hyaluronidase.
