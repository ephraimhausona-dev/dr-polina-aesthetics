From the journal

Why is having a prescribing practitioner essential?

Filler, especially in lip enhancements, should be placed by someone trained to prevent and manage complications.

  1. Overfilled or misplaced filler

    When too much is used or it is injected at an incorrect depth, causing an unnatural appearance.

  2. Infections or reactions

    Rare, but poor sterility can lead to infection at the injection site.

  3. Accidental vascular injection

    Very rare, yet a medical emergency if not spotted and dealt with correctly. Practitioners should have specific training in using hyaluronidase.
